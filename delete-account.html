MealFrame — Account & Data Deletion

Last updated: October 2026

MealFrame (Google Play package com.lensbite.app) is developed and published by Prokot. Contact: autoattend.app@gmail.com.

This page explains how to delete your MealFrame account and the data associated with it. MealFrame is an AI-powered food calorie analyzer. You can delete your account from inside the app, or — if you no longer have the app installed — by email.

Option 1 — Delete from within the app

  1. Open MealFrame.
  2. Go to Settings → Account → Delete Account.
  3. Confirm the deletion.

This immediately and permanently removes your account and its data, as described below.

Option 2 — Request deletion by email

If you’ve uninstalled the app or can’t sign in, email autoattend.app@gmail.com from (or mentioning) the email address linked to your MealFrame account, with the subject “Delete my account.” We will process the request and confirm by email.

What data is deleted

  • Your Firebase authentication account (your name, email, and user ID)
  • All of your scan history and food-analysis results stored in our database (Firestore)
  • Your subscription association with our systems
  • Locally stored data (calorie goal, water settings, on-device history) is removed when you uninstall the app

What is kept, and for how long

  • Food photos are never stored on our servers — they are processed for analysis and discarded.
  • Anonymous, hashed rate-limit counters contain no personal information and cannot identify you; they expire automatically.
  • Aggregated, anonymised analytics that cannot identify you may be retained.

Processing time

In-app deletion is immediate. Email requests are completed within 30 days.

Contact

Questions about deletion or your data: autoattend.app@gmail.com
